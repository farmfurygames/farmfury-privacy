Farm Fury - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 8, 2026

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how "Farm Fury — Last Beast Standing" ("the Game") handles information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information directly. The Game does not require account creation, login, or any personal data entry. All game progress is stored locally on your device only.

However, third-party services integrated into the Game may collect certain information automatically:

2.1 Google AdMob (Advertising)

AdMob may collect:

  • Device advertising identifier (Ad ID)
  • IP address
  • Device information (model, OS version, screen size)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • App usage data and interaction with ads

See Google's Privacy Policy.

2.2 Firebase Crashlytics (Crash Reporting)

Crashlytics may collect:

  • Anonymous session ID (randomly generated, not linked to any identity)
  • Device model and manufacturer
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Crash stack traces and error logs

No personally identifiable information is collected.

See Firebase Privacy.

2.3 Google Analytics for Firebase

Analytics may collect:

  • Anonymous app-instance ID (generated by the Firebase SDK; not the crash-report session ID)
  • Automatically collected app events (e.g. first open, session start, app exception)
  • Device type and OS version
  • Country and language settings

The Game does not send any custom gameplay events.

No personally identifiable information is collected.

2.4 Google Play Games Services (Leaderboard)

If you sign in, Play Games Services may collect:

  • Google Play Games player ID
  • Game progress submitted to the leaderboard (your best wave / high score)

Used solely to display and rank your score on the optional leaderboard. Sign-in is handled by Google and can be declined; the Game works fully without it.

Additionally, at launch the Game contacts worldtimeapi.org once to verify your device clock. This request discloses your device's IP address to that service; no other data is sent and nothing is stored.

3. How Information Is Used

  • Advertising: To display ads and measure performance
  • Analytics: To understand usage patterns and improve the Game
  • Crash Reporting: To identify and fix crashes

4. Personalized vs. Non-Personalized Ads

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your advertising consent through an IAB TCF v2.2 consent form on first launch. You may allow or decline personalized advertising; your choice is passed to AdMob as a standard TCF consent string. You can change it at any time in Settings > "Ad Preferences", which re-opens the same form. Outside these regions, personalized ads are shown by default and you can opt out in your device's advertising settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads).

5. App Store Rating Prompt

The Game may occasionally invite you to rate it on Google Play. The invitation is triggered by local gameplay milestones only (sessions played, runs completed, and whether your most recent run set a personal record). The Game collects and transmits nothing as part of this prompt.

On Android the invitation uses Google Play's In-App Review feature, which is displayed by the Google Play Store app. Any rating or review you write is submitted to and stored by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We are not told whether you left a review, and the Game never rewards you for leaving one. You can permanently dismiss the prompt at any time.

6. Sharing Your Results

The run summary and victory screens include an optional "Share" button. Using it creates an image of your result on your device and passes it to the Android share menu, where you choose which app receives it. Nothing leaves the Game unless you tap Share and then pick an app.

The image and its caption contain only your in-game result (character, wave reached, enemies defeated, time survived and best combo) plus a link to the Game's Play Store listing. They contain no personal or device information. Once you hand the image to another app, that app's own privacy policy governs what happens to it. The image is written to the Game's private storage and is replaced each time you share.

7. Data Storage

All game progress is stored locally on your device. We run no servers, so it is never sent to us; the one exception is your best wave, which is submitted to the Google Play Games leaderboard when you are signed in (see section 2). Uninstalling deletes all local data.

8. Children's Privacy

The Game is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA).

9. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA / LGPD)

You may have the right to: access, delete, opt out of personalized ads, portability, and rectification of your data. Contact us to exercise these rights.

10. Device Permissions

  • INTERNET: Required for advertisements
  • AD_ID: Required by Google AdMob
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Optional — local reminder notifications only; no data collected, may be denied

Nie zbieramy żadnych danych umożliwiających identyfikację osoby.

Zobacz politykę prywatności Firebase.
